    How it measures

    The clock
    The green state is timed from inside a requestAnimationFrame callback, so the stopwatch starts on the frame the browser actually paints green — not the millisecond the timer fired. That removes a frame or two of noise.
    The input
    Taps are read from pointerdown rather than click, which fires immediately instead of waiting to see if you meant a double-tap or a drag.
    What's a good score
    Typical reaction to a visual cue is around 250 ms. Touchscreens add roughly 40–80 ms of their own scanning and processing latency on top, so phone numbers run slower than mouse numbers. Compare yourself to yourself, on one device.
    False starts
    Tapping while the screen is still red voids the round and replays it. There's no way to game the delay — it's re-randomised between 1.4 and 3.6 seconds every time.

    Everything stays on your device. Your record is kept in localStorage and never leaves the phone.
